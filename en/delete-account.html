Deleting your account and data

Chepeti

You can delete your Chepeti account and all data linked to it at any time. There are two ways.

Option 1. In the app

The fastest way, with an immediate result:

Profile → Data and privacy → Delete all my data

Option 2. Request by email

If the app is no longer on your phone or you cannot access it, send a request to support@chepeti.com.

Write from the email address you used in the app. This is the only way we can make sure that the account owner, and not someone else, is asking to delete it. If you write from another address, we will ask you to confirm access to the account.

If you know your user ID (in the app: «User ID» at the bottom of the main Profile screen, CHE- and 8 digits), include it in the email so we find the account faster. If you do not know it, that is fine: the address is enough.

We process requests within 30 days and let you know by email when the deletion is complete.

What will be deleted

  • Your pets' profiles, photos and all medical records: visits, medications, vaccinations, analyses, and the diary
  • Uploaded documents and files in the cloud (scans, photos)
  • Emergency card pages behind the QR code
  • Contacts you saved in the app
  • Your account and email
  • Sharing settings: people you shared pets with will no longer see your records

Deletion cannot be undone. After it, nobody can restore this data, including us.

What remains

Payment records: the fact and date of the purchase, the amount, and the store transaction ID. Tax and accounting law requires this. These records are kept for the period set by law and are used for nothing except accounting and confirming your purchases in case of a dispute.

The log of deletion requests (only if you asked to delete the account by email): the address the request came from, the CHE ID, the date of the request and the date it was completed. It is kept for 3 years so we can show that we completed the request on time, and then deleted automatically.

Details, including place suggestions, promo codes and friend invitations, are in the Privacy policy, section 10.

About the subscription

Deleting the account does not cancel a Premium subscription: the store manages it, not us. To avoid further charges, cancel the subscription separately in the App Store or Google Play settings.

If your pet gets lost

A QR tag that leads home

Whoever finds your pet scans the tag and sees an “I’m lost” page. You choose what it shows, and your address is never there.

  1. Create a QR in the app and print a collar tag.
  2. Pet missing? Turn on “I’m lost” mode.
  3. The finder scans the tag and calls you. You see how many times it was scanned.

FAQ

Does it work offline?

Yes. Data lives on your phone and reminders arrive without a connection. You need internet for cloud backup, sharing and the QR page.

Is there an iPhone version?

It’s on the way. Chepeti is on Google Play now, and the iPhone version is coming to the App Store.

What happens to my data if I change phones?

If you’ve added and confirmed your email, sign in on the new phone and all your records come back: visits, treatment, vaccinations, tests and diary. With Premium, your document files come back too.

Who can see my data?

Only you. Without an account everything stays on your phone; an account is only for cloud backup, QR and sharing. Analytics turn on only with your consent, and you can delete everything in one tap in the app.

Which pets does it support?

Cats, dogs, rabbits, rodents, birds, reptiles and more.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app: Profile, then “Data and privacy”. Or on the account deletion page.

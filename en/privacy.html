Privacy policy

Version 4, effective from 12 November 2026

Chepeti («we», «our») respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it and how we protect it.

1. What data we collect

We collect only the data you enter into the app yourself:

  • Pet data: name, species, age, profile photo
  • Medical records: visits, medications, vaccinations, analyses, documents
  • Diary: dated notes you make about your pet (observations, wellbeing) and photos attached to them
  • Email address: only if you add it for backup or account recovery
  • Contacts: places and vets you add to the «Contacts» section: name, address, phone, opening hours, your notes
  • Technical data: a device identifier for local storage; if you have an account, the device type (Android or iOS), app version and date of the last sign-in, so support can see which version a problem happened in
  • Data for the emergency card page: only if you create a QR yourself (see «Emergency card and QR page»): what you chose to show, the phone numbers you added to the card, and the number of scans in lost-pet mode
  • Support requests: subject, text and reply address, if you write to us from the app
  • App language: if you have an account, we write emails and notifications to you in it
  • Notification token: if you have an account, a technical identifier the server uses to send notifications to your phone about sharing, letters from the Chepeti team and invitation bonuses. It does not name you and changes when the app is reinstalled
  • Friend invitations and promo codes: if you use them, who invited whom (both CHE IDs), the date, whether the invited person paid and whether a bonus was granted; which promo codes you activated and until what date Premium is active (see the terms, section «Invitations and promo codes»)
  • City: if you enter it in Profile yourself. It is stored only on your phone: the place directory opens on it, and if you turn on partner offers, offers are matched by it (see «Advertising»)

2. How we store data

By default all data is stored locally on your device. If you added and confirmed an email, records about your pets (visits, medications, vaccinations, analyses, diary, contacts) are also stored in protected cloud storage so they can be restored on a new device, without a subscription. Document files and pet photos go to the cloud only with a Premium subscription.

We do not sell your data and do not give it to anyone for their own purposes: not to advertisers, data brokers or anyone else.

At the same time, for the app to work, we use providers that process data on our behalf and only for this:

  • Google (Firebase): cloud storage, sync and authentication; also checking that a request to our servers comes from the genuine Chepeti app (Firebase App Check: on Android via Google Play Integrity, on iOS via Apple App Attest). For this, technical attributes of the app and device are sent, without your records, documents or email
  • Amazon Web Services (Amazon SES): sending emails with confirmation codes and service notices about your account (for example, about the file storage period after the subscription ends). It processes data on servers in the European Union (Ireland). Only your email address and the text of the email reach it: it has no access to records about your pets, documents or contacts
  • Apple App Store and Google Play: processing subscription payments. We do not see or store your card details: payment happens entirely on the store's side
  • Expo (app update service): delivering app updates outside the store. On launch the app asks whether an update is available, and Expo receives the IP address, the app version and a persistent technical installation identifier (it changes only when the app is reinstalled). Expo does not receive records about your pets or your email
  • GitHub Pages (Microsoft): hosting of our website chepeti.com and of service files the app reads on launch and when you return to it (maintenance notice), as well as the list and images of partner offers. Like any website, GitHub sees the IP address of the request; the app sends nothing else there
  • Google Maps: the map in the place directory and in the place form in contacts. Google sees which area of the map you view and the IP address. An address you type into «Show on map» is sent to the phone's geocoder (on Android this is a Google service) to find the point on the map. Your location for «Near me» is determined by the phone and is not sent to our server

Google (Firebase), Amazon and the stores may not use your data for their own purposes. Expo, GitHub and Google Maps receive only the technical request data listed above and process it under their own privacy policies.

Where your data is stored. Records about your pets (visits, medications, vaccinations, analyses, diary) are stored on servers in the European Union. Document files and photos, as well as technical data (authentication, de-identified diagnostics), are stored and processed on Google infrastructure in the United States. Email addresses to which confirmation codes are sent are processed in the European Union (Ireland).

Data is transferred outside the European Union on the basis of the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission. This is a recognised mechanism that Google applies to all its customers and that obliges it to provide a level of protection comparable to the European one.

3. Legal basis for processing

The law requires us to explain why exactly we are allowed to process your data. The bases differ by purpose:

  • For the app to work: pet records, documents, contacts, sync. Basis: performance of the contract with you, that is, these terms of use. Without this data the app cannot do what you installed it for.
  • Analytics and error reports: basis: your consent. You can withdraw it at any time in Profile.
  • Non-personalised advertising without Premium: basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the free version of the app. Personalised Google advertising: only with your consent in Google's form.
  • Matching partner offers to your pets: basis: your consent (off by default). You can withdraw it at any time in Profile.
  • Building the place directory: basis: our legitimate interest in a complete and accurate directory. How it works is described in the directory section.
  • Emergency card page behind the QR: basis: performance of the contract. The page appears only when you create a QR yourself and shows only what you chose.
  • Support requests: basis: performance of the contract (to answer and help with the app).
  • Payment records: basis: compliance with an obligation set by tax and accounting law.
  • Protection against abuse: limiting the number of code requests, detecting an abnormal number of file actions, and technical logs of such events. Basis: our legitimate interest in protecting the service and its users from abuse and excessive costs. These logs contain no records about your pets and are kept for a limited time.

4. Analytics and diagnostics

With your consent (which you can change in Profile → Data and privacy) we may collect de-identified app usage data and error reports. For this we use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics by Google.

This data shows which screens are opened most often and where the app crashes (including when sync, a file upload or a purchase check failed: only the technical error code), and contains no records about your pets, documents or the content of your contacts. It may include a device identifier: a technical number that tells one device from another but does not name you.

If you do not consent, the app works the same: this data is simply not collected.

5. Advertising

Without Premium the app shows an ad banner. It is shown by the Google AdMob ad network. To show and count ads, Google receives technical device data: the advertising identifier, device type, language and approximate location by IP address. Google does not receive records about your pets, documents or contacts. With Premium there are no ads, and AdMob does not start.

Personalised or not. In countries where the law requires it (European Union, United Kingdom, Switzerland), Google shows its own consent form before the first ad. If you do not agree, ads become non-personalised, that is, not matched to your interests. You can change your choice at any time: Profile → Data and privacy → Ad settings. On iPhone the system also asks for permission to track, and without it the advertising identifier is not sent.

Partner offers for your pets. We may also show offers from our partners, such as clinics, pet shops or pet food makers, marked «Ad». We will match them to you (by your pet's species, age and sex and the city you entered) only if you turn this on yourself in Profile → Data and privacy. Matching is off by default. Matching happens on your phone: the app gets the list of offers and picks a suitable one itself, and this data does not leave the device and is not sent to us or to partners. Partners see only the total number of views and clicks, without data about people. We never match offers by your pet's illnesses, medications or analyses. If matching is off, partner offers are shown without matching, the same for everyone. You can turn matching off at any time in the same place.

6. Directory of places on the map

The app has a directory of veterinary places with a map. We compile it ourselves: we look for places, check the data on their websites and in open directories, and add them to the map.

To make the directory more complete and accurate, we may use data about places (clinics, grooming salons, pet shops, pet hotels) that you save in the «Contacts» section: name, address, phone, opening hours, website.

This data is used solely to check it against open information about places. We compare it with our directory and public sources, add our own research where needed, and add the place to the map.

  • Contacts of private individuals, including individual vets, are not used for this: data about a specific person cannot be checked against open sources about places, and we do not do it
  • Your personal notes on contacts are not used: they are not data about the place
  • Before a place appears in the directory, we check the data on its website or in open directories. Data from your contacts is a reason to check, not a ready entry
  • The directory publishes only what the place says about itself publicly: name, address, phone, opening hours, website. There is nothing about you or about which place you go to
  • This data is not used for anything else: we do not contact places on your behalf and do not show other users who goes where

7. Sharing a pet

You can give another person access to your pet's records. Then they see this pet's medical records: visits, medications, vaccinations, analyses and documents. You open the diary separately: it is closed by default.

  • Only you open access. We do not show your data to anyone without your action.
  • Access can be revoked at any time; after that the person no longer sees the records.
  • We cannot affect what they managed to see, save or copy while access was open.

8. Emergency card and QR page

For each pet you can create a QR to hang on its collar. Whoever scans the QR sees a public page at p.chepeti.com in the browser with data from the emergency card. No app is needed for this.

  • The page appears only when you press «Create QR» yourself. Without that, your data is shown to no one.
  • What is shown. Always: name, photo, species, breed, colour, age, sex and neutering. The rest you choose yourself: «Important» (allergies, intolerances, chronic illnesses, current medications, blood type, note for the vet), «Identification» (chip number, pet passport, weight), «Phone» (numbers you added to the card) and «Insurance» (insurer, policy number, insurer's phone; hidden by default). Your address and email are never on the page.
  • Lost-pet mode. If you turn it on, the page shows that the pet is being searched for, and a button to call your number from the card. In this mode the number is shown even if the «Phone» section is off, because otherwise nobody could call you.
  • Who can see it. Anyone who has the QR or the link. The page address contains a random code that cannot be guessed, and we forbid search engines to index it. But if the QR hangs on a collar, anyone nearby can scan it, so show only what you do not mind a stranger seeing.
  • What we learn about the person scanning. Nothing that points to a person. Only in lost-pet mode we count the number of scans and the time of the last one, so you see it in the app. Like any website, the server on Google infrastructure (Firebase) processes technical request data, such as the IP address, to deliver the page. We do not store it or link it to a person.
  • How to remove it. «Turn off the page» deletes it immediately. «Reissue QR» deletes the old page and creates a new address, and the old QR stops working. The page is also deleted together with the pet or the account. Changed data on the page updates within a minute.
  • An account is needed to create a QR: our server keeps a copy of the page. It is stored in the European Union, and the pet's photo for the page is stored in Google storage, like other photos (see section 2).

9. Subscription and payments

Subscription payment happens entirely on the App Store or Google Play side. We do not see or store your card details.

From the store we receive and keep only the purchase confirmation: the fact and date of payment, which plan was bought, the period, the subscription status (active, renewing, cancelled, payment problem) and the transaction identifier. This is needed to give you the paid access, restore it on a new device and confirm your purchase in case of a misunderstanding.

The subscription is linked to your Chepeti account (confirmed email), not to the phone. We check each purchase directly with the store and reconcile its status once a day to reflect renewals, cancellations or refunds in time. For this we keep the history of subscription status changes.

10. How long data is kept and what happens on deletion

Your pet's data is kept while you use the app. You can delete a single record, a pet or the whole account at any time: Profile → Data and privacy. If the app is already deleted from the phone, send a request; how to do it is described on the Deleting your account page.

When you delete the account, we delete your records, documents and photos, as well as the emergency card pages behind the QR. This cannot be undone. Deleting the account does not cancel the subscription in the App Store or Google Play: cancel it there, otherwise the store will keep charging.

Deleted cloud documents (with Premium) first stay in the trash for 30 days, where they can be restored, and are then deleted permanently. While a file is in the trash, it takes space in your cloud storage. Deleted records disappear from your other devices immediately, and we remove the service deletion marks from the server within 60 days.

We are obliged to keep part of the data even after the account is deleted: tax and accounting law requires it. This concerns payment records: the fact and date of purchase, the amount, the store transaction identifier. Such records are kept for the period set by law and are used for nothing except accounting and confirming your purchases in case of a dispute.

What remains after the account is deleted, besides payment records.

  • The log of deletion requests, if you asked to delete the account by email: the address the request came from, the CHE ID, the date of the request and the date it was completed. It is kept for 3 years (the general limitation period) so we can show that we completed the request on time, and then deleted automatically. If you delete the account yourself in the app, there is no such entry.
  • Place suggestions and promo code activations remain without your address, CHE ID or the account's technical identifier: a suggestion keeps only the data about the place, an activation keeps only the date and period (so the code's activation count stays correct). Processed suggestions are deleted after a year.
  • Friend invitations: if a bonus was already granted for your invitation, an entry remains with the technical identifier of the deleted account, without email or CHE ID, so that the annual bonus limit of the person who invited you is counted correctly. Other invitation entries are deleted; in the entries of people you invited you are no longer named.

If a Premium subscription ends. Records about your pets are kept; the medical history is not affected. Cloud copies of files (scans and photos) are kept for 3 more months and then deleted; you see the exact date in the app, and we warn you in advance. During this time you can download all files to the device for free (a bit more than your whole archive per month; a file already downloaded without a subscription is given again only to the same device; in the last month before deletion there is one extra top-up so you can make it), and renewing the subscription cancels the deletion. Files already on your phone are never affected.

Support requests sent from the app are kept for a year, so we can see the history of your question, and then deleted automatically. Emails sent directly to our address are kept in our mailbox.

Server technical logs contain the account's technical identifier (without email, pet records or texts you write) and are kept for a limited time: 30 days by default. Anonymous diagnostics are kept for a limited time and do not name you.

11. Your rights (GDPR)

If you are in the EU or Ukraine, you have the right to:

  • Get a copy of your data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete all your data (Profile → Data and privacy → Delete all my data)
  • Withdraw consent to processing of optional data at any time
  • Complain to the data protection supervisory authority in your country if you believe we have violated your rights

We may update this policy. We will notify you about significant changes through the app.

So we can neither send you a copy of this data nor delete it on your request: we simply do not have it.
